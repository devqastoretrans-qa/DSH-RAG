## 1. Logging In & Accessing Your Account

1. Go to Dr. Sarah Hensley's official website (`https://doctorsarahhensley.com`).
2. Select the **Login** / **My Account** link in the top navigation menu.
3. Enter the **email address** and **password** you used at checkout.
4. Once signed in, open your **Course Dashboard / My Courses** page to view all enrolled masterclasses, programs, and downloadable workbooks.

> **Tip:** If you purchased a course as a guest or cannot find a newly ordered program, verify that you are signed in with the exact email address used on your order confirmation.

---

## 2. Resetting a Forgotten Password

1. Open the website **Login** / **My Account** page.
2. Click **Forgot Password** (or **Lost your password?**) below the sign-in form.
3. Enter your registered account email address and submit the form.
4. Check your inbox (and spam/promotions folder) for the password reset email and follow the link inside to choose a new password.
5. Return to the **Login** page and sign in with your updated password.

---

## 3. Finding Courses, Modules, Lessons & Workbooks

1. Sign in to your account and navigate to your **Courses / Learning Portal Dashboard**.
2. Click the title of the enrolled program or masterclass you want to open.
3. Use the course curriculum outline to move sequentially through **Modules** and **Lessons**.
4. Look inside the lesson body or lesson **Materials / Downloads** section for PDF **Workbooks**, reflection exercises, or audio/video replays.
5. Select **Download** to save PDF workbooks locally to your phone or computer.

---

## 4. Billing, Receipts, Subscriptions & Account Settings

1. Sign in and open **My Account**.
2. Select **Orders** to view past purchases and download order receipts/invoices.
3. Select **Subscriptions** or **Payment Methods** (for active memberships or payment plans) to update a card or manage renewal settings.
4. Select **Account Details** to update your display name, email address, or account password.

---

## 5. Getting Help & Submitting Support Requests

If a course does not appear in your dashboard after purchase, a download link fails, or your reset email never arrives:

1. Confirm you are logged in under the same email used at checkout.
2. Refresh your browser or try a private/incognito window to rule out cached login sessions.
3. Use the **Contact / Support** page on `https://doctorsarahhensley.com` and include:
   - Your full name and checkout email address
   - Your WooCommerce order number (if applicable)
   - The course or portal page you were trying to open and a short description or screenshot of any error message.
